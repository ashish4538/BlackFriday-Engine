Frequently Asked Questions

How does the Flash Sale work?

Items are available in limited quantities. Once stock reaches zero, the item is sold out.

Does placing an order collect payment?

An accepted order reserves one item. Payment is not collected.

Can I return an item?

Due to the nature of Flash Sales, all sales are final.
